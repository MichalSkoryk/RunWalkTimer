Build a steady running base with simple walk/run intervals.

Base Pacer lets you set separate walking and running durations, then repeat them for a total workout time or a chosen number of cycles. Clear countdowns keep the current phase and overall time easy to check at a glance.

Keep your rhythm

• Enable a metronome separately for walking and running
• Set each tempo from 70 to 180 BPM
• Adjust the active BPM while the workout is running
• Choose from built-in transition, completion, and metronome sounds
• Turn transition cues off whenever you prefer

Stay in control

• Start, pause, resume, skip, or stop a workout
• Use background notification controls while the screen is off
• See the current Walk or Run phase, interval time, and total time remaining
• Finish after a chosen duration or number of cycles

Simple and private

• No account required
• Workout configuration and sound preferences stay on your device
• No ads
• Optional tips do not unlock features

Base Pacer is designed for Android 10 and newer.
