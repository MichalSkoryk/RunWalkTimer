Base Pacer: Run/Walk Timer

Privacy policy

Effective July 29, 2026

Base Pacer is designed to run without an account, advertising, or analytics. Your workout configuration and timer state stay on your Android device.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Base Pacer: Run/Walk Timer Android application (the “App”), developed by Michał Skoryk.

Data collection

The App does not require an account and does not collect or transmit personal data, health data, location, workout history, device identifiers, or analytics to the developer or to third parties. The App does not contain advertising or tracking SDKs.

Information stored on your device

To operate and recover an active timer, the App stores a small amount of application data in Android's private app storage:

  • walk and run durations, workout goal type, total duration or interval count;
  • metronome settings and BPM values, sound-cue settings, and selected built-in sounds; and
  • the current workout status and timing checkpoints needed to keep or restore a user-initiated workout.

This local information is not sent off your device. The App does not read GPS routes, steps, heart rate, contacts, photos, or other files on your phone.

Android features and permissions

The App uses these device features only to run the timer:

  • Foreground service and notifications: keep a workout countdown running when the App is in the background or the screen is locked, show the current phase and remaining time, and provide Pause, Resume, Skip, and Stop controls.
  • Wake lock: keep the active timer and metronome accurate while the screen is off. It is released when no longer needed.
  • Audio and vibration: play the selected local transition, completion, and metronome sounds and provide local vibration cues.

These features process timer state locally and are not used to collect personal or health information.

Sharing and selling data

The App does not share or sell personal data or health data. Because workout information is not transmitted to the developer, there is no remote workout database associated with the App.

External links

If you choose Support, the App asks for confirmation and then opens buycoffee.to in your external browser. The privacy-policy link also opens this website in your browser. External websites may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. The App does not receive payment details, account information, or transaction status from buycoffee.to.

Retention and deletion

Local settings and timer state remain on your device until they are updated, Android clears them, or you delete them. You can remove all App data through Android Settings by opening Apps → Base Pacer → Storage → Clear storage, or by uninstalling the App. Since the App does not maintain remote user records, there is no separate server-side deletion process.

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users.

Changes to this policy

This page may be updated if the App's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top will identify the latest version.

Contact
